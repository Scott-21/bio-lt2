6 The Cell Cycle

The ordered series of events: grow → copy DNA → prepare → divide. Daughter cells are genetically identical to the parent.

🟣 G1 — "Growth"

  • Cell grows, metabolically active
  • Organelles copied
  • NO DNA replication

🟢 S — "Synthesis"

  • DNA is replicated
  • 1 chromosome → 2 sister chromatids joined at the centromere
  • Centrosome duplicated (animal cells)

🔵 G2 — "Get Ready"

  • Final growth & preparation
  • Checks conditions
  • 2 centrosomes present

🟡 G0 — Exit

  • Metabolically active, but no division
  • Quiescence (reversible), terminal differentiation & senescence (irreversible)
⚠️ Exam trap
DNA replication doubles the DNA — NOT the chromosome number. Sister chromatids still share one centromere, so a cell entering mitosis is still "46 chromosomes" worth of count.

Mitosis — P → PM → M → A → T

💡 Remember
Cytokinesis: animal = contractile ring → cleavage furrow (actin + myosin) · plant = cell plate from Golgi vesicles → middle lamella. Interphase = ~90% of the cycle and it is not a resting phase!

7 Chromosome Structure & Karyotyping

~2 meters of DNA fits into a nucleus under 10 μm thanks to packaging with histone proteins.

📦 Packaging order
DNA → Nucleosome → 30-nm fiber → 300-nm fiber → Chromatid → Metaphase chromosome · A nucleosome = DNA wrapped around histones · Chromatin = DNA + associated proteins

Karyotyping = sorting + arranging + identifying chromosomes by number, size, shape, centromere position, and banding. Humans: 46 = 22 autosome pairs + 1 sex pair.

🔍 Reading a karyotype — in this order

  • 1. NUMBER — 46? 47? 45?
  • 2. Pairing · 3. Size · 4. Structure/centromere position
  • 5. Sex chromosomes — XX or XY?
⚠️ Exam trap
Given an unknown karyotype, examine the NUMBER of chromosomes first. 45,X → Turner (female, missing X) · 47,XXY → Klinefelter (extra X).

8 Sexual Life Cycles & Meiosis

Meiosis makes haploid (n) gametes from a diploid (2n) cell: 2n = 46 → n = 23, and fertilization restores 46. One DNA replication, two divisions, four haploid cells.

Synapsis → tetrad → crossing-over → chiasmata. Five substages:

🎯 The critical distinction
Meiosis I = homologous chromosomes separate (reductional) · Meiosis II = sister chromatids separate (equational, like mitosis). Metaphase I = independent assortment → genetic diversity.

9 Accidents in Cell Division

Three big ways things go wrong: loss of cell-cycle control (cancer), separation errors (nondisjunction), and breakage (structural aberrations).

🚗 Proto-oncogene vs. Tumor suppressor

  • Proto-oncogene = accelerator (normal growth) → mutation = stuck-on oncogene
  • Tumor suppressor = brake → mutation = brakes fail

🍞 Benign vs. Malignant

  • Benign: stays at original site, not cancerous
  • Malignant: invades + metastasizes (spreads to distant sites)
💡 Why cancer is hard to treat
Tumors are genetically complex — different mutations → different cell populations → different treatment responses. Modern fix: sequence the tumor → personalized therapy.

Nondisjunction → Aneuploidy

Failure to separate: Meiosis I error = homologues fail to separate · Meiosis II/mitosis error = sister chromatids fail to separate. Results in monosomy (2n−1) or trisomy (2n+1).

Structural aberrations (number problem vs. material problem)

✂️ Deletion
A segment is lost. Cri-du-chat syndrome = deletion of the short arm (p) of chromosome 5 → high-pitched cat-like cry, small head, wide-set eyes, developmental delay.
🔀 Translocation
Segment moves to a non-homologous chromosome. Philadelphia chromosome, t(9;22) → BCR::ABL1 fusion gene → chronic myelogenous leukemia (CML). The issue is the arrangement of genetic material, not the number.
🔗 Robertsonian translocation (Down carrier)
Part/all of chromosome 21 fuses with chromosome 14. Carrier is phenotypically normal (balanced) but can produce unbalanced gametes → increased risk of a child with Down syndrome.

⚡ One-Page Cheat Sheet

  • Cell cycle: G1 grow → S copy DNA → G2 get ready → M divide · G0 = exit (still metabolically active)
  • Mitosis: P condense → PM envelope breaks → M middle → A apart (sister chromatids) → T two nuclei
  • Cytokinesis: animal = cleavage furrow (actin+myosin) · plant = cell plate → middle lamella
  • Packaging: DNA → nucleosome (histones) → 30-nm → 300-nm → chromatid → metaphase chromosome
  • Karyotype: 46 = 22 pairs + sex pair · check NUMBER first
  • Meiosis: 1 replication, 2 divisions, 4 haploid cells · crossing-over = pachytene (Prophase I)
  • Meiosis I = homologues separate · Meiosis II = sister chromatids separate
  • Sperm = 4 products, equal, starts puberty · Egg = 1 product + 3 polar bodies, unequal, Meiosis II finishes at fertilization
  • Cancer: proto-oncogene = accelerator · tumor suppressor = brake · benign stays · malignant metastasizes
  • Nondisjunction → aneuploidy: monosomy 2n−1 / trisomy 2n+1 · 13 Patau · 18 Edwards · 21 Down
  • Sex chromosome: 45,X Turner · 47,XXY Klinefelter · 47,XYY Jacobs · 47,XXX Triple X
